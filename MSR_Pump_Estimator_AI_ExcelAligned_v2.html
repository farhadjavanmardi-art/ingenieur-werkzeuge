Pump Estimator | Formulas, Assumptions, Traceability

Scope:
Pumps (20–100 kW) + drive (DOL / Softstarter / ABB VFD) + protection + cabling + PLC/DCS interface. The tool generates a defensible BOM and exports Excel-style Einzelpositionen and Summe Rev03.
Signal rules (strict):
  • Signal class is only XE or XI.
  • DIDO = DI + DO:
    • XE → Interposing relay per DIDO channel
    • XI → IS switching barrier/isolator per DIDO channel
  • AIO = AI + AO:
    • XI → IS analog barrier/isolator per AIO channel
I/O model (per pump): 
BASIC:
  DO: Start/Enable (1)
  DI: Fault (1)

STANDARD:
  DO: Start/Enable (1)
  DI: Fault (1) + Run feedback (1)

ADVANCED (typical VFD):
  DO: Start/Enable (1)
  DI: Fault (1) + Run (1) + Ready (1)
  AO: Speed setpoint (1)
  AI: Speed feedback / current (1)
Cost model: 
Material = sum(BOM lines)
Montage  = (€/pump × pumps) + (€/m × (powerCableMeters + instrCableMeters))
IBS      = €/loop × loopCount
Programming = €/channel × totalChannels

LoopCount rule = max(pumps, channels) (or chosen in sidebar)
LoopDrawings = €/loop × loopCount

Planning Basic  = BasicHours × BasicRate
Planning Detail = DetailHours × DetailRate
Planning Extern = (Hours×Rate) or (% of Basic+Detail)

Handling fee applies to Material + Montage - a procurement markup belongs on
purchased goods, not on your own hours.
Contingency applies to EVERYTHING: material, montage, commissioning, planning,
programming, FAT/SAT, loop drawings and the handling fee. Hours are less certain
than material prices, not more.
If any line still carries an UNKNOWN or RFQ price, the grand total is marked
(vorlaeufig) and the amount resting on unsecured prices is shown next to it.
Price updates (reality check):
Standalone HTML cannot reliably scrape vendor sites (CORS, bot protection, logins). This tool tracks OK / UNKNOWN / RFQ_REQUIRED and generates RFQ emails instead of inventing numbers.
