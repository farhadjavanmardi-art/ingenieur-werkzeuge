Instrumentation Estimator | Formulas, Assumptions, Traceability

What you asked for:
A real estimator that produces a defensible BOM, respects XE / XI rules, and outputs Excel-like Einzelpositionen + Summe structures so your company spreadsheet doesn’t laugh at you.
Signal rules (strict):
  • Signal class is only XE or XI.
  • DIDO channels = total (DI + DO).
    • XE → Interposing relay per DIDO channel
    • XI → IS switching barrier/isolator per DIDO channel
  • AIO channels = total (AI + AO).
    • XE → no barrier added by default
    • XI → IS analog barrier/isolator per AIO channel
Excel alignment (your Rev03 file):
Your workbook uses:
  • Summe Rev03: Planning Basic/Detail/Extern, Material, Montage, IBS, Total per area (I/C/M/etc.)
  • Einzelpositionen: rows with (Bereich, Kalk.Modul, Beschreibung, Kontierung, Anzahl, Total all-in)
This estimator generates both and can export a matching XLSX.
Cost model: 
Material = sum(BOM lines)
Montage = (€/device × devices) + (€/m × total cable meters)
IBS     = €/loop × loopCount
Programming = €/channel × totalChannels  (exported under C08)

LoopCount rule = (selected in sidebar)
LoopDrawings = €/loop × loopCount (exported under I01)

Planning Basic  = BasicHours × BasicRate
Planning Detail = DetailHours × DetailRate
Planning Extern = (Hours×Rate) or (% of Basic+Detail)

Handling fee applies to Material + Montage - a procurement markup belongs on
purchased goods, not on your own hours.
Contingency applies to EVERYTHING: material, montage, commissioning, planning,
programming, FAT/SAT, loop drawings and the handling fee. Hours are less certain
than material prices, not more. A contingency that skips half the sum makes the
offer too cheap - and too cheap is what gets signed.
If any line still carries an UNKNOWN or RFQ price, the grand total is marked
(vorlaeufig) and the amount resting on unsecured prices is shown next to it.
Price updates:
Standalone HTML cannot scrape vendor sites reliably (CORS, bot protection, logins). So the tool tracks OK / UNKNOWN / RFQ_REQUIRED and generates RFQ emails instead of making up numbers.
